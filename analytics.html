Service

Analytics

Simple, plain-language insights, so you always know what's actually working, not just a wall of numbers.

What This Actually Means

Analytics means turning raw traffic and campaign data into a monthly answer to one question: what's actually working? Not a dashboard full of numbers you have to decode yourself, but a plain-language summary that tells you where to spend time and budget next.

What's Included

Traffic & source tracking

Know where visitors actually come from, not just how many showed up.

Conversion tracking

See which pages and campaigns turn visitors into leads or customers.

Dashboard setup

One place to see the numbers that matter, without digging through multiple tools.

Goal tracking

Measured against what actually matters to your business, not vanity metrics.

Plain-language reports

Monthly summaries in real terms — traffic, leads, what changed — not jargon.

Actionable recommendations

Every report ends with what to do next, not just numbers.

Who This Is For

  • Businesses running ads or campaigns without knowing what's working
  • Anyone confused by Google Analytics on their own
  • Brands wanting decisions based on data, not guesses

What You Walk Away With

  • One dashboard instead of five disconnected tools
  • Monthly reports you can actually read
  • A clear view of which channels are worth the spend
  • Recommendations, not just raw numbers

Common Questions

Do I need Google Analytics already set up?

No — setup is part of the service if you don't have it.

How often do I get reports?

Monthly by default, with more frequent updates available for active campaigns.

What if the numbers show something isn't working?

That's exactly the point — we adjust the plan based on what the data shows, instead of sticking to something that isn't performing.
