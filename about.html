About

Marketing that sounds like you, not a template.

Aaru Digi Spark is a one-person digital marketing practice built on a simple idea: small brands don't need a bigger agency, they need clearer decisions.

The short version

I started Aaru Digi Spark after watching too many good small businesses lose momentum to marketing that looked busy but wasn't going anywhere — inconsistent posting, no clear brand voice, and no real read on what was actually working.

Focused scope

Six services, chosen because they reinforce each other, not because they fill a rate card.

Direct communication

You talk to the person doing the work — no account managers relaying messages.

Honest reporting

If something isn't working, you hear that in week two, not month three.

Built to hand off

Everything is documented so you're never locked in — the goal is your growth, not dependency.
