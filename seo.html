SEO

Improving how easily people find you on Google — through keywords, technical fixes, and content that actually answers what they're searching.

What This Actually Means

SEO means making your site easier for search engines to understand, and your content easier for real people to find when they're already looking for what you offer. It's not a trick or a hack — it's fixing what's broken, writing for the right keywords, and being patient while it compounds.

What's Included

Keyword research

Finding the exact phrases your customers are already searching.

On-page fixes

Titles, headings, and page structure cleaned up for search engines.

Technical audit

Site speed, mobile usability, and crawlability checked and fixed.

Local SEO

Getting your business showing up correctly in local search and maps.

Content optimization

Rewriting existing pages so they actually rank for what they're about.

Plain-language reporting

Rankings explained in terms of traffic and leads, not jargon.

Who This Is For

  • Local businesses wanting to show up in "near me" searches

Common Questions

How long until I see results?

SEO is gradual — typically two to four months for meaningful movement, longer for competitive terms.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No one honestly can. I focus on measurable improvement in traffic and visibility instead of empty promises.

Is this a one-time fix or ongoing?

Technical fixes are largely one-time; ranking and content work benefits from ongoing attention.
