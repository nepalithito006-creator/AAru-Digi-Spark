Consultation

A direct, practical conversation about your specific marketing question — without a sales pitch attached.

What This Actually Means

Consultation is time set aside just to think through your situation out loud with someone who does this for a living. No package, no upsell pressure — just clear, honest input on what to do next, whether or not you hire me for anything else.

What's Included

Single session

A focused hour to work through your specific marketing question.

Pre-session questionnaire

So the time is used efficiently instead of spent on discovery.

Audit + notes

A written summary of what to fix first, second, and later.

Follow-up resource list

Tools and next steps tailored to what came up.

Ongoing check-ins

Optional monthly calls to keep decisions on track.

No-pressure upsell

Advice that stands on its own, whether or not you hire me further.

Who This Is For

  • Business owners unsure where to start with marketing
  • Anyone wanting a second opinion on their current strategy
  • People overwhelmed by conflicting advice online

What You Walk Away With
