Service

Branding

A visual identity built with purpose — one that still feels like you as you grow.

What This Actually Means

Branding is more than a logo — it's the color, type, and voice that makes your business recognizable whether someone sees a post, a business card, or your storefront. Done right, it stays consistent even as you add more channels and more people to your team.

What's Included

Logo & mark

A clean, versatile logo that works from a favicon to a storefront sign.

Color & type system

A small, deliberate palette and type pairing, not fifty options.

Brand guide

A one-page reference so anyone can stay on-brand without asking you.

Applied templates

The identity applied to social, print, and web so it's ready to use.

Brand voice guidelines

How your brand sounds in writing, not just how it looks.

Stationery & assets

Business cards, social kit, and email signature, as needed.

Who This Is For

  • New businesses starting from scratch
  • Brands that have outgrown a DIY logo
  • Anyone whose visuals look different across every platform

What You Walk Away With

  • A logo and identity that works at any size
  • A one-page guide anyone on your team can follow
  • Consistency across social, print, and web
  • Source files, not just a PNG you can't edit

Common Questions

How many logo concepts do I get?

Typically two to three directions to choose from, refined from there.

What files do I receive?

Full source files plus web-ready formats (PNG, SVG) for everywhere you need them.

Can you refresh an existing brand instead of starting over?

Yes — refreshes keep what's working and fix what isn't, rather than starting from zero.
